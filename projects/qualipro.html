QualiPro

Quality Management • Inspections • NCR / Corrective Actions

Summary

QualiPro is a quality management platform designed to support inspections, audit readiness, and consistent corrective action tracking. It structures inspection data, evidence capture, and non-conformance workflows into a single system so teams can maintain traceable quality records and produce clean management reports.

  • Structured inspections and checklists
  • NCR tracking with status visibility
  • Corrective actions with accountability tracking
  • Evidence capture (photos/attachments) and audit trails
  • Reporting dashboards for issues and trends

At a glance

Who it’s for
  • Project/site teams running recurring inspections
  • Quality managers needing auditable NCR tracking
  • Leadership needing visibility into repeat issues and trends
What it solves
  • Unstructured inspection notes and missing evidence
  • NCRs lost in email threads or spreadsheets
  • Delayed corrective actions with weak accountability
  • Limited reporting on repeat failures and quality hotspots

Problem

Quality breaks down when evidence is inconsistent and corrective actions aren’t tracked end-to-end. This creates repeat issues, weak audit readiness, and reactive problem solving.

Solution

QualiPro standardises inspections into structured checklists, captures evidence consistently, and tracks NCRs and corrective actions through clear statuses and reports.

Key workflows

  • Create inspection templates/checklists
  • Perform inspections and capture findings + evidence
  • Log NCRs and assign corrective actions
  • Track statuses and accountability to closure
  • Review dashboards and reports for trends and repeat issues

Notable features

  • Structured checklists and inspection templates
  • NCR logging with categorisation and severity support
  • Corrective action tracking with status and accountability
  • Attachment support for evidence and audit trails
  • Reporting views for management and compliance

Tech / Architecture

  • Web app with structured database-backed records
  • Workflow and reporting views designed for auditability

Challenges & decisions

  • Making checklists flexible across contexts without losing structure
  • Ensuring evidence capture is fast on site
  • Keeping workflows strict enough for audits but usable in reality

Results / impact

  • More consistent inspection and quality records
  • Clear accountability on corrective actions
  • Better readiness for internal reviews and audits
