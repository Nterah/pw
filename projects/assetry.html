Assetry

Asset Register • Geofencing • QR/NFC • Renewals • GRAP-Aligned Reports

Summary

Assetry is a full asset register with geofencing, QR/NFC scans, renewal reminders, and GRAP-aligned reporting. Ideal for organisations that need a clean, auditable inventory.

  • Asset onboarding with photos & documents
  • QR/NFC scan history and location verify
  • Renewals & depreciation schedules; insurance packs

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask • React • REST/JSON • GIS (optional) • Scheduled jobs • Export to CSV/PDF.
