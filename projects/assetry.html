Assetry

Asset register • QR/NFC • Renewals • Insurance • GRAP-aligned

Summary

Assetry is a mobile-first asset register for municipalities and SMEs. Capture assets with QR/NFC, attach documents and photos, track locations and conditions, manage renewals/warranties, and export insurer-friendly schedules. Reporting supports GRAP-aligned categories and depreciation where required.

  • QR/NFC tagging with scan audit trails
  • Renewal reminders and service history
  • Bulk import/export, multi-site structures

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask • React • REST/JSON • background schedulers (renewals) • CSV/Excel import/export.
