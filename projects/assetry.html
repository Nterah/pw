Assetry

GRAP-Aligned Asset Register • Verification • Renewals & Replacement Planning

Summary

Assetry is a South Africa–aligned asset register platform designed to track assets end-to-end (acquisition to disposal), maintain audit-ready verification records, and support GRAP-aligned reporting. It’s built for real-world asset complexity — vehicles with mileage, IT assets with accessories, and software licences with renewals — and produces clean exports for financial reporting and insurance schedules.

  • Full asset lifecycle register (acquisition → transfers → disposals)
  • Verification/audit workflows (audit trails, reconciliations)
  • Renewals & replacement tracking (warranties, licences, reminders)
  • Asset relationships (accessories, peripherals, linked items)
  • Reporting-ready exports (finance + insurance use cases)

At a glance

Who it’s for
  • Asset managers and operations/facilities teams
  • Finance teams preparing GRAP-aligned reporting
  • Organisations needing insurance-ready asset lists and renewal planning
What it solves
  • Spreadsheets that don’t scale or audit well
  • Missing links between primary assets and accessories
  • Renewals and warranties falling through the cracks
  • Time-consuming audits and reconciliations

Problem

Asset registers fail when assets aren’t uniform: computers include accessories, vehicles require mileage tracking, and software renews over time. Spreadsheets can’t reliably track relationships, evidence, and verification history across multiple locations and custodians.

Solution

Assetry structures asset data into a lifecycle-ready system: assets, linked items, custodian/location history, renewals, maintenance, and verification events are all tracked. Reports support finance, audit, insurance, and replacement planning.

Key workflows

  • Register assets with structured categories, valuation, custodian and location
  • Link accessories/peripherals/software to primary assets where relevant
  • Capture verification and movement events (transfers, checks, disposals)
  • Track renewals (licences, warranties, service intervals) with reminders
  • Produce exports for finance reporting and insurance schedules

Notable features

  • Lifecycle management (acquire, transfer, verify, maintain, dispose)
  • Support for non-uniform asset types (vehicles, IT assets, software licences)
  • Verification records designed for traceability
  • Renewal and replacement planning via reminders and reporting

Tech / Architecture

  • Web app designed for structured asset data + verification trails
  • Workflow + reporting views aligned to ops and finance needs

Challenges & decisions

  • Modeling asset relationships without making capture painful
  • Building verification workflows that are audit-ready but practical
  • Keeping reports clean and usable across finance/insurance contexts

Results / impact

  • Improves confidence in asset register completeness and verification
  • Reduces reconciliation effort through structured tracking
  • Supports finance reporting, insurance schedules, and proactive renewals
