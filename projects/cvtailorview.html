CVTailorView

Tender CV Generator • Multi-Tenant • Smart Matching & Exports

Summary

CVTailorView is a multi-tenant platform for building and maintaining a structured CV database (internal staff and external consultants), then generating tender-ready outputs. It supports rich personnel profiles, project experience tracking, requirement-based matching, and document generation (CV packs and supporting tender assets) with consistent branding and templates.

  • Structured CV/personnel database with detailed profile tabs
  • Project experience tracking (multi-role support, durations, evidence)
  • Tender workflow + requirement capture + match indicators
  • Word/PDF export using templates and branding
  • External CV capture via secure link for consultants

At a glance

Who it’s for
  • Engineering/consulting firms responding to tenders
  • Bid teams needing consistent CV formats and fast assembly
  • HR/BD teams maintaining staff and consultant profiles
What it solves
  • Tender prep time wasted rebuilding CVs repeatedly
  • Inconsistent CV formats and missing compliance evidence
  • Weak search/matching when selecting the right personnel
  • No central repository for projects, skills, affiliations, documents

Problem

Tender submissions require strict CV formats and evidence (qualifications, registrations, years of experience, relevant projects). When info lives across emails and documents, teams lose time and increase the risk of compliance failure.

Solution

CVTailorView centralises personnel information into a structured database. It supports tender workflows that define requirements, shortlist best matches, and generate submission-ready documents with consistent branding and template-based exports.

Key workflows

  • Onboard a company tenant (branding, defaults, lookup tables)
  • Add internal personnel with structured profile tabs
  • Track projects/experience with roles and durations
  • Create a tender lead and capture requirements
  • Run matching to shortlist candidates with match indicators
  • Generate CV packs and supporting exports (Word/PDF + structured outputs)

Notable features

  • Multi-tenant onboarding with branding and default configuration
  • Deep profile tabs (bio, education, employment, skills, affiliations, attachments)
  • Advanced search across skills, roles, projects, experience and other filters
  • External CV capture portal (secure access)
  • Document generation across multiple output formats and templates
  • Import/export utilities and admin settings for scaling

Tech / Architecture

  • SaaS-style tenant isolation + role-based access
  • Export pipelines for Word/PDF with template controls
  • Structured lookup tables for consistency (roles, clients, types, statuses, skills)

Challenges & decisions

  • Designing onboarding that’s quick but supports deep configuration
  • Balancing data quality with usability across many CV fields
  • Building matching logic that supports bid decision-making without noise

Results / impact

  • Faster tender preparation using reusable structured CV data
  • Improved submission consistency with templates and branding controls
  • Better shortlisting confidence through match indicators and advanced search
