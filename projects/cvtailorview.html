What’s included
  • Profiles: personal data, education, employment, skills, affiliations, languages.
  • Projects: client, value, status, dates, role + fall-back roles, rich descriptions.
  • Advanced search: role, duration, client, project type/status, value range, keyword (description only).
  • Exports: paragraph/bullet/table layouts; headshot & signature; company branding; timeline page.
Business impact
  • Faster tender turnaround with consistent branded exports.
  • Higher match quality via role grouping and timeline context.
