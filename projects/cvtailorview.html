Summary

End-to-end CV management for engineering teams: profiles, roles, skills, timelines and project libraries—exported to branded Word templates or PDFs.

  • Advanced search with filters (role, duration, value, keywords)
  • Template-based exports with branding (DOCX/PDF)
  • Timeline charts for project history

What’s included

  • Profiles: personal data, education, employment, skills, affiliations, languages.
  • Projects: client, value, status, dates, role + fall-back roles, rich descriptions.
  • Advanced search: role, duration, client, type/status, value range, keyword (description only).
  • Exports: paragraph/bullet/table layouts; headshot & signature; company branding; timeline page.

Business impact

  • Faster tender turnaround with consistent branded exports.
  • Higher match quality via role grouping and timeline context.

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask (REST/JSON) • React • DOCX/PDF export pipeline • Role-based access.
