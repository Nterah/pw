ZiMaSy

Invoicing • Statements • Collections • Exceptions

Summary

ZiMaSy automates invoicing workflows and month-end statements. Data is normalised from spreadsheets, exports or the accounting system, then validated and posted to customers with an audit trail.

  • Batch statement generation & delivery
  • Exception finder (missing PO, price variance, overdue items)
  • Dispute notes & collection follow-ups
  • Downloadable packs for finance & auditors

Problem

Manual statement prep and error-prone invoice checks slowed collections and created rework at month-end.

Approach

Built parsers for CSV/XLSX, applied business rules (tax, rounding, PO policy), and automated statement bundling and email delivery with status tracking.

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask • React • REST/JSON • Background jobs (RQ/Celery) • Mail API integration • Audit logging.
