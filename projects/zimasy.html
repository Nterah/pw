ZiMaSy

Field Operations • Attendance & Verification • Payment Readiness

Summary

ZiMaSy is a mobile-first system designed for field-based programmes where attendance, verification, and accurate payment preparation are essential. It structures beneficiary/participant records, captures evidence-backed daily activity, and provides reporting that reduces fraud risk and improves accountability.

  • Beneficiary registration and verification workflows
  • Attendance capture with evidence (GPS/photo/timestamps)
  • Approval flow to support payment readiness
  • Operational dashboards and exception reporting
  • Mobile-first design for real field conditions

At a glance

Who it’s for
  • Programme administrators managing large beneficiary lists
  • Field supervisors verifying attendance and proof-of-work
  • Finance/admin teams preparing accurate payment runs
What it solves
  • Manual, error-prone attendance registers
  • Weak evidence and auditability (disputes/fraud risk)
  • Slow approvals and inconsistent payment readiness
  • Poor visibility into site-level field activity and exceptions

Problem

Field programmes often rely on paper registers or scattered spreadsheets. This creates weak evidence trails, inconsistent approvals, and payment disputes — especially when multiple sites, supervisors, and beneficiaries are involved.

Solution

ZiMaSy provides a structured workflow from registration through attendance capture and verification. Records are captured with auditable signals (location/time/attachments where needed), approvals are tracked cleanly, and reporting surfaces anomalies before payment preparation.

Key workflows

  • Register beneficiaries with required identity and programme details
  • Capture daily attendance/activity with verification evidence
  • Supervisor review and approval of submissions
  • Admin reporting (missing days, anomalies, site summaries)
  • Finance uses approved records to support payment preparation

Notable features

  • Beneficiary master data management
  • Daily attendance/activity logs with verification signals
  • Status tracking (submitted → reviewed → approved)
  • Exception reporting (anomalies, missing submissions)
  • Audit-friendly records designed for governance and accountability

Tech / Architecture

  • Mobile-first web app patterns (PWA-friendly approach)
  • Structured database-backed records for traceability
  • Reporting views for operational monitoring and governance

Challenges & decisions

  • Balancing strict verification with usability for field users
  • Designing evidence capture that works in low-connectivity settings
  • Keeping workflows simple while remaining audit-grade

Results / impact

  • Improves confidence in attendance records and payment readiness
  • Reduces fraud risk through stronger verification trails
  • Gives programme leadership clearer operational visibility
