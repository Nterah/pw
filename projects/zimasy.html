ZiMaSy

Workflow • Document control • Approvals • SLA & audit trails

Summary

ZiMaSy streamlines document routing, approvals, and retention. Teams configure steps, roles, and SLAs; documents move automatically with reminders and escalations. Versioning and signatures create an audit-ready record for compliance.

  • Configurable flows by department with due dates & escalations
  • Version control, change logs, and read-receipts
  • Dashboards for SLA adherence and bottlenecks

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask • React • REST/JSON • Background jobs for reminders and escalations. Exportable trail for audits.
