TAMS360

Transport Asset Management • Inspections • GIS Mapping • Condition Analytics

Summary

TAMS360 is a mobile-first asset/inspection platform. Crews capture standardized records with photos, GPS coordinates, and condition ratings—online or offline. Supervisors view live maps and dashboards to plan work and verify completion.

  • QR/NFC scans, offline queueing, photo attachments
  • Map-based assignments and route guidance
  • Operational dashboards and CSV/PDF exports

At a glance

Who it's for

  • Municipalities and transport infrastructure teams
  • Field teams conducting routine inspections
  • Management needing condition analytics for prioritisation

What it solves

  • Fragmented asset data and inconsistent inspection notes
  • Difficulty converting defects into comparable condition metrics
  • Weak traceability and audit trails
  • Low visibility into network condition and hotspots

Problem

Transport infrastructure decisions need consistent condition assessment. When inspections are ad hoc, defect data isn’t comparable, priorities are disputed, and it becomes hard to justify maintenance planning.

Solution

TAMS360 standardises inspections and scoring, structures evidence capture, produces condition metrics, and provides map-based and dashboard visibility so organisations can prioritise interventions confidently.

Key workflows

  • Onboard a tenant, configure users/roles
  • Maintain asset inventory records
  • Conduct inspections in the field (defects + evidence)
  • Generate condition metrics/indices from defect scoring
  • Review dashboards and maps to prioritise interventions

Notable features

  • Structured defect capture and inspection workflows
  • Calculation engine for condition scoring/indices
  • GIS mapping with filters and clustering for scale
  • Offline storage patterns for field capture with sync approach
  • Audit logs and notifications for governance

Tech / Architecture

  • Frontend: React + TypeScript, Tailwind, charting + GIS via Leaflet
  • Backend: PostgreSQL-based storage + functions/APIs, tenant-aware auth
  • Offline: local storage patterns for field use

Challenges & decisions

  • Keeping field capture fast while preserving structured analytics data
  • Ensuring scoring logic stays explainable to stakeholders
  • Designing mapping views that remain usable at scale

Results / impact

  • More consistent inspections and condition assessment
  • Decision-ready analytics for maintenance planning
  • Stronger governance through audit trails and structured evidence
