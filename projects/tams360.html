TAMS360

Mobile Asset Capture • Inspections • GIS • Dashboard

Summary

TAMS360 captures field assets on mobile, records inspections with photos, and maps them for planning and maintenance. Designed for municipalities and utilities.

  • Offline-friendly mobile capture with required fields
  • GIS layers: points/lines/polygons with styles & filters
  • Condition scoring, interventions, and export packs

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL/PostGIS • Flask • React • REST/JSON • Leaflet/Mapbox • Media storage • PDF/CSV exports.
