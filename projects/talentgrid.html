TalentGrid

People Analytics • Performance • Compensation • Learning • Leave • Utilisation

Summary

TalentGrid unifies HR data into one live analytics layer—headcount, performance, compensation, learning, leave, and utilisation—so HR and business leaders can see risk and opportunity at a glance.

It includes consistent model definitions, drill-downs to the individual record, and exportable board-ready views.

  • Single source of truth across HR domains
  • Equity & comp analytics (range breaches, compa-ratio, pay-for-performance)
  • Learning & skills tracking aligned to roles
  • Workforce utilisation, leave trends, and attrition risk indicators

Problem

HR metrics lived in disconnected spreadsheets and tools, making it hard to answer basic questions fast and consistently.

Approach

Designed a canonical HR schema; built ingestion to standardise uploads; modelled metrics (performance distributions, comp ratios, learning progress, leave balances) and exposed curated dashboards.

Architecture / Stack

PostgreSQL • Flask API • React • REST/JSON; auth with role-based access; scheduled loads; audit trails; export to CSV/PDF.

Results

  • Exec-level HR reporting time cut from days to minutes
  • Consistent analytics across performance, pay, and utilisation
