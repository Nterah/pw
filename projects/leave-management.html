Leave Management

Leave Requests • Approvals • Balances • Calendar Planning

Summary

Leave Management Tool is a lightweight leave tracking and approvals system built around a structured workflow. It supports employee records, leave types and policies, leave requests, approvals, leave balances, and calendar views — designed for teams that want process control and visibility without a heavy HR suite.

  • Leave requests + approval workflow
  • Auto-updated leave balances and summaries
  • Calendar view for planning and coverage
  • Basic reporting and auditability
  • Lightweight and easy to deploy

At a glance

Who it’s for
  • Small-to-mid teams without dedicated HR platforms
  • Admin/HR staff who need a clear approvals trail
  • Managers who need quick visibility into team availability
What it solves
  • Leave requests scattered across chats and emails
  • Manual balance calculations and errors
  • No single calendar for leave planning
  • Weak approval records and audit trails

Problem

Without a structured tool, leave management becomes fragmented: requests are scattered, balances drift, and teams struggle with planning and operational cover.

Solution

This tool centralises leave into one workflow: capture requests, approve/reject with status tracking, automatically maintain balances, and show leave calendars and reports for visibility.

Key workflows

  • Admin configures leave types and policy rules
  • Employees submit leave requests (type, dates, notes)
  • Managers approve/reject with audit-friendly tracking
  • Leave balances update automatically as requests are processed
  • Calendar and reports support planning and oversight

Notable features

  • Employee master list + entitlements
  • Leave request intake + approvals tracking
  • Balance calculations + summaries
  • Calendar views for upcoming leave
  • Reporting views for HR/admin review

Tech / Architecture

  • Spreadsheet-style structured workflow with standardised tabs/sections
  • Automation-friendly (notifications/approval actions where applicable)

Challenges & decisions

  • Keeping the workflow robust under multi-user editing
  • Preventing “version drift” with standardised data entry rules
  • Ensuring approvals remain traceable and auditable

Results / impact

  • Faster leave processing with less manual admin
  • Better planning with a single calendar view
  • Reduced balance calculation errors
