## 🎯 Executive Summary

**ProVisio** is a comprehensive, cloud-based business management platform designed specifically for engineering and professional services firms in South Africa. It replaces fragmented tools (Excel, QuickBooks, Expensify, email) with one integrated system.

### Core Value Proposition
- **Save 20+ hours/week** on administrative tasks
- **Real-time visibility** into projects, budgets, and cashflow
- **100% SARS-compliant** expense and vehicle management
- **Automated approval workflows** for timesheets, expenses, and leave
- **Project-centric cost tracking** for accurate profitability

### Current Status
- **✅ 100% MVP Complete** - All 12 core modules fully functional
- **✅ Production-Ready** - Deployed and database-connected
- **✅ Multi-tenant Architecture** - Scalable for multiple firms
- **✅ Role-Based Access Control** - 5 roles (Admin, Finance, PM, Supervisor, Staff)

## 🏢 Target Market & Problem Statement

### Primary Target Audience

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Industry** | Engineering consulting (Civil, Structural, Mechanical, Electrical), Architecture, Quantity Surveying, Project Management |
| **Firm Size** | 5-150 employees (sweet spot: 10-50 employees) |
| **Geography** | **Primary:** South Africa<br>**Secondary:** International expansion-ready |
| **Business Model** | Project-based professional services |
| **Pain Points** | Drowning in spreadsheets, budget overruns, compliance issues, manual processes |

### The Core Problems We Solve

#### Problem 1: **Spreadsheet Hell** 📊
**Current State:**
- Timesheets collected in Excel, WhatsApp, and email
- Friday afternoons spent manually consolidating 20+ individual timesheets
- Version control nightmares ("Budget_FINAL_v3_REALLY_FINAL.xlsx")
- Copy-paste errors causing thousands in financial mistakes
- No single source of truth

**Cost:**
- **15-25 hours/week** wasted on manual data entry and consolidation
- **R 50,000-100,000/year** in errors and rework
- Delayed invoicing = delayed cash collection

#### Problem 2: **Budget Surprises** 💸
**Current State:**
- Budget tracking in disconnected spreadsheets
- Project costs not linked to actual timesheets and expenses
- Discover projects are 30% over budget only at month-end
- No visibility into which projects are profitable
- Client invoicing delayed while hunting for billing data

**Cost:**
- **20-30% project budget overruns** due to lack of real-time tracking
- **15-20 days delayed invoicing** = cashflow problems
- Lost profitability on 40% of projects

#### Problem 3: **Compliance Chaos** 🔥
**Current State:**
- Vehicle allowance rates manually looked up (and often wrong)
- SARS rejects claims because rates are outdated
- Missing receipts in WhatsApp threads
- VAT calculations done manually with frequent errors
- No audit trail for tax compliance

**Cost:**
- **R 30,000-60,000/year** in rejected SARS claims
- **20+ hours/month** chasing receipts and fixing VAT errors
- Risk of SARS audits and penalties

#### Problem 4: **Approval Bottlenecks** ⏳
**Current State:**
- Expense claims submitted via email/WhatsApp
- Supervisors lose track of what needs approval
- No visibility into approval status
- Employees chase reimbursements for weeks
- Leave requests lost in email threads

**Cost:**
- **10-15 days average** expense reimbursement time
- Employee frustration and disengagement
- Double-approvals or missed approvals

#### Problem 5: **Cashflow Blindness** 💰
**Current State:**
- No real-time view of money coming in vs going out
- Cashflow forecast in manual spreadsheet (updated weekly at best)
- Surprise cashflow crunches
- Don't know which clients owe money until month-end
- Missing creditor payment deadlines

**Cost:**
- **Cashflow crises** requiring emergency loans
- Late payment fees and damaged supplier relationships
- Inability to plan growth investments

- **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** - Cloud database
- **Supabase Auth** - User authentication
- **Supabase Storage** - File/attachment storage
- **Supabase Realtime** - Live updates/notifications
- **PostgREST** - Auto-generated REST API

### Database Architecture:
- **Multi-tenant Schema:** `tenant_id` on all tables for data isolation
- **Row-Level Security (RLS):** PostgreSQL policies enforce tenant boundaries
- **Audit Trails:** `created_at`, `updated_at`, `created_by`, `approved_by` columns
- **Soft Deletes:** `is_active` flag instead of hard deletes (preserve history)

### Security:
- **AES-256 encryption** at rest and in transit
- **Row-Level Security (RLS)** policies on all tables
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** with 5 roles
- **OAuth 2.0** authentication (Google, Microsoft)
- **POPIA compliance** (South Africa data protection)
- **Daily backups** with 30-day retention

### Hosting & Deployment:
- **Recommended:** Vercel (free tier available)
- **Alternative:** Netlify, AWS, DigitalOcean
- **CDN:** Global edge network for fast loading
- **SSL:** Auto-issued certificates (HTTPS)
